Game Instructions:
Sunshine/Scene 1
Left click with mouse on the clothes on the left. This will add them to the character.
Left click with mouse on the "Remove!" button to remove article of clothing.
Left click with mouse on the "Ready!" button to move on to dressing the next character up.
Left click with mouse on the "Add Info" button to add information about the character to what will be shown in console and written to DateResults file.
Bryan/Scene 2
Left click with mouse on the clothes on the left. This will add them to the character.
Left click with mouse on the "Remove!" button to remove article of clothing.
Left click with mouse on the "Add Info" button to add information about the character to what will be shown in console and written to DateResults file.
Left click with mouse on the "Go Back!" button to go back to the previous scene.
Left click with mouse on the "Ready!" button to signify the two of them are ready for their date.
